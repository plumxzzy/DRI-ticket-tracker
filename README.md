# 🪄 DRI Utils

## Introduction
**DRI Utils** is a comprehensive set of web-based utility tools designed for players looking to optimize their gameplay, manage their resources, and track their progress efficiently. It features two primary tools wrapped into a sleek, dynamic, and easy-to-use interface: the **DRI Rune Calculator** and the **DRI Ticket Tracker**.

Whether you're calculating the exact multipliers on your runes or predicting how long it will take to unlock your next gamepass, DRI Utils takes the guesswork out of the grind.

## Features

### DRI Rune Calculator
* **Comprehensive Rune Data:** View exact details for both Main and Event runes, including stats, costs, and multipliers.
* **Max Calculation:** Calculates the precise number of runes required to hit the MAX level for any specific boost.
* **Gain Simulator:** Input your current *Rune Luck, Bulk, Speed*, and *Clone* stats to simulate drop rates.
* **Time Estimations:** Automatically estimates the time required to max out a rune based on your simulation parameters.

### DRI Ticket Tracker
* **Smart Bundle Advisor:** Analyzes your currently owned gamepasses to determine if purchasing the Gamepass Bundle or Potion Bundle is worth the Robux.
* **Gamepass Predictor:** Set your daily grind hours and current ticket count to calculate exactly when you'll unlock your target gamepass.
* **Multi-Account Support:** Easily swap between up to 3 different profiles (Main, Secondary, Friend) to track multiple accounts at once.
* **Dynamic Achievements:** Unlock tracking achievements as you complete goals and save Robux!
* **Detailed Analytics:** Track gamepasses completed, total Robux saved, remaining tickets, and your overall completion progress.

## How to use

1. **Access the Tool**: Open the `index.html` file in any modern web browser to launch the web app.
2. **Switching Tools**: Use the toggle buttons at the top of the page to switch between the **Rune Calculator** and the **Ticket Tracker**.
3. **Using the Rune Calculator**:
* Select a category (Main or Event) and choose a Rune Pack.
* Click on any rune from the grid to view its max stats and required counts.
* Adjust the Gain Simulator inputs (Luck, Bulk, Speed, Clone) to see real-time estimates of your hourly/daily gains.
4. **Using the Ticket Tracker**:
* Select your active profile from the dropdown.
* Input your current tickets and daily playtime.
* Check off the gamepasses you already own in the table.
* Select your Target Gamepass to see exactly how long the grind will take.
* Review the **Smart Bundle Advisor** to see if buying a bundle will save you Robux!
